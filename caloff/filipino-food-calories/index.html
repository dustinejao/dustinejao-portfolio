Calories in Filipino food

By Dustine Jao, maker of CalOff. Updated 27 November 2026.

A cup of cooked rice is about 260 kcal. Chicken adobo is about 462 kcal a serving, a bowl of sinigang na baboy about 420 kcal, and a tapsilog plate about 735 kcal.

Below: calories, protein, carbs and fat for 139 Filipino dishes, at the servings you get at home or at a carinderia.

These are the reference values built into CalOff, the offline calorie counter, per serving. Recipes, cooks and cuts of meat vary, so treat each figure as a good estimate rather than a lab measurement. Rice is listed separately: add about 260 kcal for every cup that comes with an ulam.

Lighter swaps that still taste like home

  • Rice is usually the biggest number on the plate. Two cups is about 520 kcal, more than most ulam. Going from two cups to one saves more than any swap below.
  • Sinigang na hipon over sinigang na baboy: about 262 instead of 420 kcal a bowl.
  • Inihaw na tilapia over lechon kawali: about 260 instead of 585 kcal, with more protein.
  • Chicken inasal over crispy pata: about 390 instead of 800 kcal.
  • Pinakbet or chopsuey on the side: about 153 and 180 kcal, against 278 kcal for laing, where the coconut milk adds up.
  • Plain rice over sinangag at breakfast: about 260 instead of 330 kcal a cup.

Where the numbers come from

Each dish is a per-100 g reference built from standard nutrition tables and typical home recipes, multiplied by an everyday serving weight. The figures are the same ones CalOff uses when you search for a dish or correct a photo scan, so a meal logged in the app matches this page. If a dish here looks wrong for how you cook it, tell me; corrections go into the next update.

Questions

How many calories are in a cup of rice?

A cup of cooked white rice, about 200 g, has about 260 kcal. Brown rice is close at 246 kcal, and a cup of sinangag (garlic fried rice) is about 330 kcal because of the oil.

How many calories are in chicken adobo?

About 462 kcal for a 220 g serving, without rice. Pork adobo is higher, about 583 kcal, because pork belly carries more fat. Add 260 kcal for each cup of rice.

How many calories are in sinigang?

A 350 g bowl of sinigang na baboy (pork) is about 420 kcal. Sinigang na bangus is about 332 kcal and sinigang na hipon (shrimp) about 262 kcal.

How many calories are in tapsilog?

A plate of tapsilog (beef tapa, garlic rice and a fried egg) is about 735 kcal. Longsilog is about 822 kcal and tocilog about 788 kcal. Most of the difference is the meat; the sinangag is around 330 kcal on every plate.

How many calories are in pancit canton?

About 438 kcal for a 250 g serving. Pancit bihon is about 400 kcal and palabok about 475 kcal.

How many calories are in lechon kawali?

About 585 kcal for a 150 g serving, most of it from fat (51 g). Crispy pata is about 800 kcal a serving.

How many calories are in halo-halo?

A glass of halo-halo is about 435 kcal, depending on how much leche flan, ube and evaporated milk go on top.

How many calories are in pandesal?

One pandesal is about 90 kcal. Spanish bread is about 204 kcal a piece and ensaymada about 304 kcal.

How many calories are in balut?

One balut is about 111 kcal, with 8 g of protein.
