Three ways to log

However the food arrives, it is logged in seconds.

A plate, a packet or a craving you already know the name of. Each one takes a few taps, and all three work with the radio off.

Snap the plate

An AI model on your iPhone names the dish, estimates the portion and returns calories, protein, carbs and fat. Rice, a viand and a drink come back as separate rows.

Scan the label

Point the camera at a nutrition facts panel and CalOff reads the printed numbers. Or scan the barcode on a packaged food. No lookup server involved.

Search 9,000 foods

USDA reference data, everyday dishes and nearly 200 Filipino favourites, all on the phone. Type a few letters, tap, set the portion in grams, millilitres or servings.

Your plan

Your targets, built in a minute.

Answer a few questions about your body and your goal. CalOff sets a daily calorie target, protein, carb and fat targets, and the date you can expect to reach your goal weight.

Works offline

Airplane mode? Basement gym? Still counting.

Most AI calorie apps send your photo to a server and wait. CalOff does the work on the iPhone, so it is as fast on a flight as it is on Wi-Fi.

  • Photo scanning runs on the phone's own chip.
  • Nutrition labels are read on the device.
  • Barcodes are looked up in a catalogue that ships with the app.
  • Food search covers 9,000+ foods with no connection.
  • History, targets and your watch never wait on a server.

The internet is used twice: a one-time 728 MB scanner download before your first photo scan (Wi-Fi recommended), and Apple's check when you buy or restore CalOff Pro.

Questions

Answers, up front.

What is CalOff?

CalOff is an AI calorie counter and macro tracker for iPhone that works offline. Snap a photo of a meal, scan a nutrition label or barcode, or search 9,000+ foods, and CalOff counts the calories, protein, carbs and fat on the phone. There is no account, and meal photos never leave the iPhone. It is made by Dustine Jao in Manila, Philippines.

Does CalOff work offline, without internet?

Yes. Photo scanning, nutrition label reading, barcode lookup and food search all run on the iPhone, so CalOff works in airplane mode. It needs the internet twice: once to download the scanner model (about 728 MB) before your first photo scan, and when you buy or restore CalOff Pro. More on offline use.

How much does CalOff cost?

CalOff is free to download and needs CalOff Pro to use. In the US: $4.99 a week, $9.99 a month, $39.99 a year, or $129.99 once for lifetime access. In the Philippines: ₱29 a week, ₱59 a month, ₱299 a year, or ₱999 once. The yearly and monthly plans start with a 7-day free trial; the weekly plan with a 3-day trial.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The yearly and monthly plans include a 7-day free trial and the weekly plan a 3-day trial, for eligible new subscribers. CalOff sends a reminder two days before the trial ends. Cancel in your Apple Account settings at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you are not charged.

Do I need an account to use CalOff?

No. There is no sign-up, email or login. Your food log, photos and health profile are stored on your iPhone.

Are my meal photos uploaded?

No. Photos are analysed by an AI model running on the iPhone and are never uploaded. CalOff's App Store privacy label lists one data type, Purchases, and it is not linked to you. Nothing is used to track you.

How accurate is CalOff's photo calorie count?

A photo gives an estimate, not a measurement: hidden oil, a deep bowl or an unusual recipe can throw it off. CalOff shows the estimate for review before anything is logged. You can rename the dish, adjust the portion, and add sauce, oil or rice as separate items, and CalOff remembers your corrections. For packaged food, scanning the nutrition label reads the printed numbers instead. How to get better photo estimates.

Does CalOff know Filipino food?

Yes. The built-in database includes nearly 200 Filipino dishes, from adobo, sinigang and kare-kare to silog plates, kakanin and street food, with everyday serving sizes. The on-device model was chosen partly because it names dishes like sinigang na bangus instead of calling them soup. See the calories in 139 Filipino dishes.

Can CalOff scan barcodes and nutrition labels?

Yes, both offline. Label scanning reads the calories and macros printed on a nutrition facts panel. Barcode lookup checks a built-in catalogue of packaged foods from Open Food Facts; if a product is not in it, scan its label instead.

Does CalOff work on Apple Watch?

Yes. The Apple Watch app shows calories left and today's macros, and CalOff has watch complications and iPhone home-screen and Lock Screen widgets.

Is CalOff available on Android?

Not yet. CalOff is on the App Store for iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later.

How does CalOff calculate my daily calorie target?

CalOff uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for resting energy, multiplies it by an activity factor from 1.2 to 1.725, then subtracts 500 kcal a day to lose weight or adds 350 to gain. Targets never go below 1,200 kcal for women or 1,500 for men, and the goal date assumes about 7,700 kcal per kilogram, capped at 1 kg a week. The app cites each source. The same maths runs in the free calorie calculator.

How is CalOff different from Cal AI?

Both count calories from a photo. CalOff runs the recognition on the iPhone, works in airplane mode and needs no account. Cal AI's App Store privacy label lists identifiers and usage data that may be used to track you; CalOff's lists only purchases, not linked to you. Full comparison.

Is CalOff medical advice?

No. CalOff is a food diary and an estimator, not a medical device. Talk to a health professional before changing your diet, especially if you are pregnant, take medication or have a history of disordered eating.
