Calories in lomi

By Dustine Jao, maker of CalOff. Updated 27 November 2026.

Lomi has about 480 kcal per bowl (400 g), with 26 g protein, 60 g carbs and 15.2 g fat. With a cup of rice it comes to about 740 kcal.

Lomi is thick egg noodle soup. That works out to 120 kcal per 100 g.

Questions

How many calories are in lomi?
About 480 kcal per bowl (400 g), or 120 kcal per 100 g. With a cup of rice, about 740 kcal.
How much protein is in lomi?
About 26 g of protein per bowl, with 60 g of carbs and 15.2 g of fat.
Can I eat lomi on a diet?
Yes, if it fits your daily calorie target. At 480 kcal per bowl, it is easier to fit with a measured portion of rice and a vegetable side. Use the calorie calculator to find your target.
