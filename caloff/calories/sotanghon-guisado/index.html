Calories in sotanghon guisado

By Dustine Jao, maker of CalOff. Updated 27 November 2026.

Sotanghon guisado has about 375 kcal per serving (250 g), with 15 g protein, 57.5 g carbs and 8.8 g fat.

Sotanghon guisado is stir-fried glass noodles. That works out to 150 kcal per 100 g.
