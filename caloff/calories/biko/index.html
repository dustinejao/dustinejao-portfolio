Calories in biko

By Dustine Jao, maker of CalOff. Updated 27 November 2026.

Biko has about 250 kcal per slice (100 g), with 3 g protein, 45 g carbs and 7 g fat.

Biko is sticky rice cake with coconut caramel. That works out to 250 kcal per 100 g.

Questions

How many calories are in biko?

Can I eat biko on a diet?
