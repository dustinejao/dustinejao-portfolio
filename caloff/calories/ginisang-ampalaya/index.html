Calories in ginisang ampalaya

By Dustine Jao, maker of CalOff. Updated 27 November 2026.

Ginisang ampalaya has about 135 kcal per serving (180 g), with 8.1 g protein, 10.8 g carbs and 7.2 g fat. With a cup of rice it comes to about 395 kcal.

Ginisang ampalaya is sauteed bitter gourd with egg. That works out to 75 kcal per 100 g.
