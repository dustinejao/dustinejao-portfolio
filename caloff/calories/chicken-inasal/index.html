Calories in chicken inasal

By Dustine Jao, maker of CalOff. Updated 27 November 2026.

Chicken inasal has about 390 kcal per serving (200 g), with 44 g protein, 4 g carbs and 22 g fat. With a cup of rice it comes to about 650 kcal.

Chicken inasal is Bacolod-style grilled chicken marinated in calamansi, lemongrass and annatto. That works out to 195 kcal per 100 g.

Keeping it lighter

  • Rice is half the plate. One cup adds 260 kcal; a second cup doubles that.
  • Leave the sauce or the fat in the pan where you can; that is where most of the extra calories sit.

Lighter dishes like it

  • kinilaw na isda: about 135 kcal per serving, 255 kcal less.
  • paksiw na bangus: about 230 kcal per serving, 160 kcal less.
  • embutido: about 250 kcal per serving, 140 kcal less.
