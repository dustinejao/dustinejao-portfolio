Calories in bistek tagalog

By Dustine Jao, maker of CalOff. Updated 27 November 2026.

Bistek tagalog has about 400 kcal per serving (200 g), with 36 g protein, 10 g carbs and 24 g fat. With a cup of rice it comes to about 660 kcal.

Bistek tagalog is beef in soy sauce and calamansi with onion rings. That works out to 200 kcal per 100 g.

Keeping it lighter

  • Rice is half the plate. One cup adds 260 kcal; a second cup doubles that.
  • Leave the sauce or the fat in the pan where you can; that is where most of the extra calories sit.
