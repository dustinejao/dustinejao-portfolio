Questions

Is CalOff a Cal AI alternative?

Yes. Both are AI calorie counters for iPhone that estimate calories and macros from a photo of a meal. CalOff runs the recognition on the iPhone itself, works in airplane mode, and needs no account.

Does Cal AI work offline?

Cal AI's App Store page does not say it works offline, and its privacy label lists photos or videos among the data it collects. CalOff analyses photos on the iPhone, so after a one-time scanner download it works with no connection at all.

Is CalOff cheaper than Cal AI?

Not on the yearly plan. On the US App Store, Cal AI's in-app purchases run from $2.99 to $29.99; CalOff is $39.99 a year, $9.99 a month or $4.99 a week. CalOff also sells lifetime access for $129.99 once. In the Philippines CalOff is ₱299 a year.

Is CalOff made by the Cal AI team?

No. CalOff is made by Dustine Jao, an independent developer in Manila, and is not affiliated with Cal AI or Viral Development LLC.
