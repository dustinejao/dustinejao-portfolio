Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

Your meal photos, nutrition log and health profile stay on your devices. CalOff has no account system or server for those records. Model downloads and purchase verification use the network as described below.

What stays on your device

The app stores or processes the following locally:

  • Meal photos. A photo you take or pick is analysed by a model running on the phone and is not uploaded. Logged meals may keep a small thumbnail in the app's private storage.
  • Your food log, including dish names, portions, calories, macros and timestamps, is written to the app's private database storage on the device.
  • Your profile, including age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal and unit preference, is used to compute your calorie and macro targets, on the device.
  • Your weight history, if you record weigh-ins.
  • Foods you save to the local food database.
  • Scan history and corrections, including model responses, corrected names and image embeddings used to remember meals.

These records are not uploaded to CalOff or a remote food-recognition service. Your platform's backup and paired-device sync settings can also apply, as described below.

Model downloads

Before the first scan, CalOff downloads the recognition model files, roughly 728 MB for the standard model, from Hugging Face, a public model host. Downloads are resumable. Choosing a different model on Android or downloading again requires another transfer.

That request necessarily reveals to Hugging Face what any file download reveals: your IP address, the time of the request and which file you asked for. CalOff attaches no identifier, no account and no information about you to it. Hugging Face's handling of that request is governed by their privacy policy. Scanning, nutrition lookup and search run locally once the model is ready. Purchase verification is a separate network operation.

Purchases and subscription verification

Apple or Google processes your payment. CalOff uses RevenueCat to verify purchases, restore access and check subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase and receipt information, and technical information such as platform, operating system, app version and locale. Network connections also expose your IP address to the receiving service.

This information supports access verification and purchase reporting. CalOff does not send RevenueCat your meal photos, food log, health profile, name or email address. Payment card details are handled by your store. See RevenueCat's privacy information. Previously verified access is cached for offline use, subject to the subscription's validity; buying or restoring requires a connection.

What we do not do

  • No meal analytics or remote food recognition.
  • No crash or performance reporting service.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers.
  • No sale of your data. Purchase verification uses the providers above.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, so CalOff never needs to ask for tracking permission.

Permissions, and why

  • Camera, to photograph a meal. The live preview runs inside the app; frames are used for the scan and nothing else.
  • Photo library, only when you choose to log a meal you already photographed, and only for the image you pick.
  • Internet (Android), for model downloads, purchase verification and links you choose to open.

You can refuse any of these. Refusing the camera or photo library means you log meals by search or by hand; the rest of the app is unaffected.

The watch and widget companions

The Apple Watch and Wear OS apps, the home-screen widgets and the Lock Screen complication display the same day's totals that the phone app already holds. Those figures are passed through Apple's Watch Connectivity or Google's Wearable Data Layer to your paired devices. The platform manages that connection and may use its own synchronization services. No CalOff server receives those totals.

Children

CalOff is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Contact us if you believe a child's purchase information requires removal.

Deleting your data

You control the local meal and health records:

  • Settings → Reset inside the app clears the food log, profile, weight history, saved foods, scan records and meal thumbnails.
  • Delete the app. iOS and Android remove the app's private storage. Device backups and original photos in your photo library are managed separately through your platform settings.

Resetting or deleting CalOff does not cancel a subscription or erase store transaction records. Manage subscriptions through Apple or Google. Contact us about purchase data held through RevenueCat; some transaction records may need to be retained by the store or provider.

Your rights

Laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA and the Philippine Data Privacy Act give you rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection over personal data. You can view, edit and delete local meal and health records in the app. Contact us for requests concerning purchase or support information; we may need transaction details to locate an anonymous purchase record.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version is posted on this page with a new effective date. A change that would materially reduce the protection described here would be announced in the app's release notes as well.
