An offline calorie counter for iPhone

By Dustine Jao, maker of CalOff. Updated 27 November 2026.

CalOff is a calorie counter for iPhone that works fully offline. Photo scanning, nutrition labels, barcodes and a 9,000-food database all run on the phone. The internet is needed once, for a 728 MB scanner download, and when you buy CalOff Pro.

Most calorie apps look offline until you try to log something: the search box spins, the photo uploads, the barcode lookup times out. CalOff was built the other way round, with the work on the iPhone and nothing to wait for.

Set it up for offline in three steps

  1. Install CalOff and start your trial while you have a connection.
  2. Download the scanner on Wi-Fi: it starts before your first photo scan, or from Settings → Scanner.
  3. Turn on airplane mode and scan a meal. That is the test, and from then on that is how it works.

Why offline matters

  • Speed. No upload, no queue, no round trip. A scan takes the same few seconds on a flight as at home.
  • Bad signal. Basement gyms, elevators, the provinces, the middle of a long flight.
  • Travel. Keep logging abroad without roaming data.
  • Privacy. A photo that never leaves the phone cannot be stored, leaked or used to train someone else's model.

Questions

Is there a calorie counter app that works without internet?

Yes. CalOff for iPhone counts calories from a photo, reads nutrition labels, looks up barcodes and searches 9,000+ foods with no connection. It needs the internet once to download the scanner model, and when you buy or restore CalOff Pro.

How big is the download?

The app is about 244 MB on the App Store. Photo scanning needs one more download of about 728 MB, the on-device AI model, before your first photo scan. It resumes if interrupted. Use Wi-Fi if your data plan is small.

Does my subscription work offline?

Yes. Once CalOff has verified your purchase it remembers it, so the app keeps working with no connection for as long as the subscription is valid. Buying or restoring needs a connection.

Does CalOff use mobile data in the background?

No. CalOff has no account, no analytics and no server for your food log, so there is nothing to sync. It uses the network only for the scanner download and for purchase checks.
