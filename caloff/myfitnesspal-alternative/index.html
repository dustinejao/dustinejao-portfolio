When MyFitnessPal is the better pick

  • You want a free tier and do not mind ads.
  • You log a lot of restaurant chains and branded foods outside the Philippines; a crowd-sourced database of that size is hard to beat.
  • You log on Android or on the web, or you rely on its connections to other fitness apps.

When CalOff is the better pick

  • You want to log by photo, fast, without typing every ingredient.
  • You are done with ads and sign-ups. CalOff has neither.
  • You want barcode scanning without paying $79.99 a year for it.
  • You log where signal is bad. Everything works in airplane mode.

Questions

Is there a MyFitnessPal alternative without ads?

Yes. CalOff has no ads and no free tier funded by them; it is paid for by subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, with a 7-day free trial. It also needs no account and works offline.

Is the MyFitnessPal barcode scanner free?

No. MyFitnessPal moved barcode scanning to MyFitnessPal Premium in 2022, and its US App Store listing shows Premium at $19.99 a month or $79.99 a year. CalOff includes barcode and nutrition label scanning, both offline.

Can I move my MyFitnessPal history to CalOff?

There is no import. CalOff starts fresh: answer a few questions and your targets are ready in about a minute. Foods you log are remembered, so your usual meals are one tap away within a few days.

Is CalOff free like MyFitnessPal?

No. CalOff is free to download but needs CalOff Pro: $39.99 a year, $9.99 a month, $4.99 a week or $129.99 once in the US; ₱299 a year in the Philippines. The yearly and monthly plans start with a 7-day free trial.
