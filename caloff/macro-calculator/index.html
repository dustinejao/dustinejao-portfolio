Questions

How do I calculate my macros?

Start from your daily calorie target, choose a split for your goal, and convert: protein and carbs have 4 kcal per gram, fat has 9. On 1,500 kcal at 35/35/30, that is about 131 g protein, 131 g carbs and 50 g fat.

What is a good macro split for weight loss?

CalOff uses 35% protein, 35% carbs and 30% fat when you are losing weight. The higher protein helps keep muscle and keeps you full on fewer calories.

How much protein do I need?

It depends on your calories and goal; on a 35% protein split, a 1,500 kcal target is about 131 g a day. Spread it across meals: eggs or tapa at breakfast, a palm-sized portion of meat or fish at lunch and dinner.

Do I have to hit my macros exactly?

No. Calories decide weight change; macros decide how you feel and how much muscle you keep. Within 10–15 g of each target on most days is plenty.
