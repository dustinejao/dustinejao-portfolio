How the calculator works

It is the same maths CalOff runs when you set it up on your iPhone, step for step, so the number here is the number the app will give you.

1. Resting energy (BMR)

The Mifflin-St Jeor equation: 10 × weight (kg) + 6.25 × height (cm) − 5 × age, then +5 for men or −161 for women.

2. Maintenance calories (TDEE)

ActivityWhat it meansFactor
SedentaryDesk job, little exercise1.2
LightExercise 1–3 days a week1.375
ModerateExercise 3–5 days a week1.55
ActiveHard exercise 6–7 days a week1.725

3. Your goal

To lose weight, CalOff subtracts 500 kcal a day; to gain muscle it adds 350 kcal; to maintain it changes nothing. The result never goes below 1,200 kcal for women or 1,500 kcal for men.

4. Protein, carbs and fat

 Share of daily calories. Grams use 4 kcal per gram of protein or carbohydrate and 9 kcal per gram of fat.

Protein sits higher while losing weight because it helps keep muscle and keeps you full for longer.

5. The goal date

The daily difference from maintenance, times seven, divided by 7,700 kcal per kilogram, gives the weekly change, capped at 1 kg a week. Your goal weight divided by that rate gives the weeks to get there.

Questions

How many calories should I eat a day?

Start from your maintenance calories: your resting energy from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, multiplied by an activity factor between 1.2 (desk job) and 1.725 (hard exercise six or seven days a week). For a 30-year-old woman who is 165 cm, 70 kg and lightly active, that is about 1,950 kcal a day. The calculator above works it out for you.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

About 500 kcal a day below your maintenance calories is the common starting point. At roughly 7,700 kcal per kilogram, that works out to about 0.45 kg (1 lb) a week at first. CalOff never sets a target below 1,200 kcal for women or 1,500 kcal for men.

What is the Mifflin-St Jeor equation?

A formula for resting energy expenditure published by Mifflin, St Jeor and colleagues in 1990: 10 × weight in kg + 6.25 × height in cm − 5 × age, plus 5 for men or minus 161 for women. It is the equation most dietitians reach for first, and the one CalOff uses.

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR (basal metabolic rate) is what your body burns at rest. TDEE (total daily energy expenditure) adds everything you do on top: walking, work, exercise. TDEE is your maintenance calories, and it is BMR multiplied by an activity factor.

Is 1,200 calories a day enough?

1,200 kcal for women and 1,500 kcal for men are the floors used in the US National Institutes of Health obesity guidelines, and CalOff will not set a target below them. Whether a given number suits you depends on your body and your health, so check with a doctor or dietitian before eating at a floor.

How many calories are in a kilogram of body fat?

About 7,700 kcal (3,500 kcal per pound) is the usual approximation, and it is the figure CalOff uses for the goal date. Real loss slows over time as the body adapts, which is why CalOff caps its projection at 1 kg a week however large the deficit.
