Support

One person reads these, the one who made CalOff. When something has gone wrong, the three things that make it fixable are your iPhone model, the iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot beats a description.

Common questions

The first scan is stuck downloading

Photo scanning needs a one-time download of the scanner model, about 728 MB on iPhone. If it stalls, it resumes from where it stopped rather than starting again. Reopen Settings → Scanner and start it once more. On a metered connection, wait for Wi-Fi. Search, labels and barcodes work while it downloads.

The calories look wrong

Correcting them is part of the flow rather than a workaround. Tap the dish name on the review screen and type what it actually was; the nutrition follows the corrected name through the local food database. Adjust the portion if the size was misjudged, and add oil, sauce or rice as their own items. What you save is what gets logged, and CalOff remembers it next time.

Scanning is slow, or the phone is warm

Recognition is real work done on your phone rather than in a data centre, so it uses battery and takes a few seconds. Every iPhone runs the same model, so the answer does not change with the hardware, only how long it takes. If it struggles, log by search: it is instant, and it is the same database.

My Apple Watch shows nothing, or yesterday's numbers

The watch app mirrors the phone; it never talks to a server. Open CalOff on the iPhone once with both devices nearby and unlocked, and the watch and its complications catch up. Check that CalOff is installed on the watch in the Watch app on your iPhone.

I switched phones and my log is gone

The log lives only on the device, which keeps it private and also means there is no cloud copy to restore. An iCloud device backup, or a direct transfer to the new iPhone, carries it across; a fresh install does not. A log that was not backed up cannot be recovered. Your purchase can: tap Restore purchase in CalOff.

How do I cancel the free trial or my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then CalOff, and tap Cancel. Do it at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends. CalOff keeps working until then. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Reset CalOff clears the log, profile, weight history, saved foods and meal thumbnails immediately. Deleting the app removes everything else with it. See the Privacy Policy for what "everything" covers.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through them at reportaproblem.apple.com. I cannot issue one directly, but tell me what went wrong. That is how it gets fixed for the next person.
