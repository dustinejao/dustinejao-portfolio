How to read the label

  • Data used to track you: may be combined with other companies' data for targeted ads or ad measurement.
  • Data linked to you: tied to your identity, usually through an account.
  • Data not linked to you: collected, but not tied to who you are.

A label tells you what is collected, not how good the app is. MyFitnessPal and Lose It! have years of features and huge food databases; Cal AI has hundreds of thousands of reviews. Questions

Which calorie counter app is the most private?

Going by the App Store privacy labels checked on 27 November 2026, CalOff declares the least: one data type, purchases, not linked to you, and nothing used to track you. SnapCalorie and MacroFactor also declare no tracking. Cal AI, MyFitnessPal, Lose It! and Foodvisor declare data that may be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

What does "data used to track you" mean on the App Store?

Apple defines tracking as linking data from an app with data from other companies' apps, websites or data brokers, for targeted advertising or measuring ads. A developer that lists data under "Data Used to Track You" says it may do that with those data types.
