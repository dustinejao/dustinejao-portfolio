How these plans are built

  • Rice stays. Lunch and dinner both have it, measured, because a plan you cannot keep is not a plan.
  • Protein at every meal: eggs, fish, chicken or munggo.
  • Soups and vegetables do the filling. Sinigang, tinola and pinakbet give volume for few calories.
  • Grilled or boiled over fried. Inihaw na tilapia and chicken inasal instead of lechon kawali.

These are sample days for healthy adults, not medical advice. Talk to a professional if you are pregnant, take medication, or have a health condition.

Questions

What is a 1,500 calorie Filipino meal plan?
Boiled eggs, pandesal and papaya for breakfast; inihaw na tilapia with a cup of rice for lunch; ginisang munggo with half a cup of rice and kangkong for dinner; and a cup of taho for merienda: about 1,506 kcal and 100 g protein.
Can I eat rice and still lose weight?
Yes. Weight follows total calories, not one food. Measure it: a cup of cooked rice is about 260 kcal, so half a cup at one meal is often all it takes to fit a target.
How many calories should I eat a day?
It depends on your body and activity. The free calorie calculator works it out with the same formula CalOff uses, and shows what to eat to lose, keep or gain weight.
Is 1,200 calories a day safe?
1,200 kcal is the lowest target CalOff will set for women, following the US NIH guidelines, and 1,500 for men. Eating at that floor is not right for everyone; talk to a doctor or dietitian first.
