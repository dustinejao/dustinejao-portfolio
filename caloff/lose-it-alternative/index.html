Questions

Is CalOff a Lose It! alternative?

Yes. Both are calorie counters for iPhone. CalOff counts calories from a photo with AI that runs on the phone, works offline, and needs no account.

Does Lose It! track you?

Its US App Store privacy label lists contact info, identifiers and usage data under "Data Used to Track You", checked on 27 November 2026. CalOff's label lists no tracking and only purchases, not linked to you.

Which is cheaper, Lose It! or CalOff?

Lose It! has a free tier, and its in-app purchases include lifetime Premium options below CalOff's $129.99 lifetime price. CalOff is $39.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or ₱299 a year in the Philippines.
