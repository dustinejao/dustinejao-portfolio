Privacy

Effective 27 October 2026

  • No account, ads or analytics. There is nothing to sign into. The app has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • Your documents stay on your phone. PaprGo renders PDFs and photos on the device. USB and Wi-Fi jobs go straight to your printer. PaprGo runs no server and uploads nothing.
  • Print with Android. If you use it, PaprGo gives a temporary PDF to the Android print service you select, then deletes its copy. That service has its own privacy policy.
  • Permissions. Network access only, to reach printers and Google Play. No storage or location permission: you pick each file in Android's file picker, and Android asks for USB access per printer.
  • Purchases. Google Play handles payment. Your free print count and purchase receipt stay on your phone and are left out of cloud backup.
  • Diagnostics, only if you send them. A report holds the printer model, USB IDs, printer capabilities, error codes, and app and Android versions. Never file names or document content.
