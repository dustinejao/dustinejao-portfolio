Listings people
watch to the end.

Real estate media is the bulk of what I do: walkthroughs, listing films and the short-form cutdowns that carry them. I cut remotely from Manila for agents and brokerages in the US and Canada, and I built the tool that does the sequencing so the turnaround is hours rather than days.

Turnaround

Back before the listing goes live.

A shoot comes in and the sequencing, clip naming and first assembly are automated, so the time I spend is spent on the cut itself rather than on paperwork.

Format

One shoot, every aspect.

The full walkthrough for the listing page, a vertical cutdown for Reels and TikTok, and a teaser for the feed. Cut from the same footage, framed for where each one lands.

Finish

Graded, mixed, delivered.

Colour, sound design and music beds are part of the job, not an upsell. Windows hold their exposure, rooms sound like rooms, and nothing clips.

Working with me

One person, whole pipeline.

Nothing gets lost between an editor, a colourist and a sound department, because there is only one of me. You send footage, you get a finished listing back.

Recent listings.

Nine recent cuts, embedded from the accounts they were posted on — the agents' and brokerages' own feeds, not mine. That is the part that matters: the work goes out under their name, on their listing, the week it was shot. The last one is a teaser cut, which is usually what goes up first.
