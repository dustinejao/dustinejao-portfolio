Words that rhyme with ilaw

Mga salitang katugma ng ilaw

By Dustine Jao, maker of Liriko. Updated 27 November 2026.

Words that rhyme with ilaw: dilaw, araw, ayaw, halimaw, ibabaw, ikaw, kalabaw.

ilaw (light; lamp) is said i·law. Words ending in -ilaw rhyme on both of its last two syllables, the strongest match. Words ending in -aw rhyme on the last syllable, the traditional tugma.

How Tagalog rhymes work

Tagalog is spelled almost the way it sounds, so a rhyme can be read off the page: split the word into syllables and compare the endings. Matching the last syllable (from its vowel to the end) is the classic tugma of Filipino poetry. Matching the last two syllables is a stronger, more musical rhyme. Words that only add an affix to the same root, like buhay and mabuhay, are left out: they repeat a word rather than rhyme with it.
