Questions

Do rhymes work offline?
Yes, that is the point of them. Both dictionaries ship inside the app, so rhymes work on a plane, in a jeepney tunnel, or with the SIM out. Nothing you write is sent anywhere to find them.
Which languages?
English and Filipino, and you can mix them in one song. The Filipino dictionary was rebuilt from an attested corpus, so every word in it is a word people actually use.
What do the rhyme colours do?
They paint the rhymes already in your song, one colour per rhyme family, so you can see the scheme you have been hearing. Turn them on from ⋯ → Rhyme colours in the editor. Multi-syllable rhymes are painted as one run across the whole phrase.
What is free, and what is Liriko Pro?
Free keeps up to ten songs and shows your single strongest rhyme for each word. Pro is a one-time ₱199 unlock that gets you every rhyme in every tier, unlimited songs, tap tempo, the full key and scale browser, beats, and the writing partner. It is not a subscription, and it never becomes one.
Where are my songs kept?
On your phone. If you turn on backup they are also copied to your own iCloud Drive or Google Drive: your account, your files. Liriko has no server holding your lyrics.
I deleted a song by accident.
Open Settings → Recycle Bin. Deleted songs wait there until you empty it.
Does the writing partner send my lyrics anywhere?
No. The model is downloaded once and then runs on the phone itself, so your song never leaves the device, which is also why it works with no signal.
I bought Pro and I am on a new phone.
Open Settings → Restore purchases. It is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account, so it comes back for free.
Something is broken.
Email digsdigg@gmail.com with your device and iOS version and what you were doing. Screenshots help more than anything else.
