What’s inside

Rhymes in two languages
English and Filipino, ranked strongest first, straight from a dictionary on the phone. No signal needed, ever.
Rhyme colours
Switch them on and every rhyme in the song lights up — one colour per family, multis painted whole.
A writing partner
Stuck on the next line? A small model that runs on your own device suggests one, in your song’s own language.
Demos where you sang them
Record a take against a beat and it stays pinned to the line it belongs to, inside the lyric.
Tempo and key
Tap out the BPM, keep a metronome running, and tag the song with the key you wrote it in.
Backed up to your own cloud
iCloud or Google Drive — your account, your files. Liriko has no server to keep your songs on.
