CastCut by Dustine Jao

Automatic multicam podcast editing for Mac.

CastCut turns your camera recordings and audio into an editable podcast timeline. It handles syncing, speaker cuts, speech cleanup and short clips, then hands the edit to Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve.

Try CastCut for Mac Visit the CastCut website

First 2 exports free · Apple silicon Mac, M1 or newer · macOS 14 or later

A podcast editor's own tool.

I'm Dustine Jao, a multimedia artist and video editor in Manila, and the maker of CastCut. I record a Taglish interview podcast with two iPhones and an audio recorder, then edit it in Final Cut Pro. Lining up recordings and choosing camera angles took up the same part of every episode. CastCut grew out of that workflow.

The goal is to start the creative edit sooner. The exported timeline stays editable, so you can change an angle, adjust a pause or keep a moment that matters to the conversation.

From recordings to an editable episode.

Sync and camera switching

Align cameras and audio by sound, then build cuts around the active speaker. Work with several cameras or create close-ups from a single wide shot.

Cleanup and short clips

Review silence and filler cuts before export. Find moments for social posts, with captions and framing for landscape, vertical and square video.

Processing on your Mac

After the initial model download, editing runs offline. Your footage stays on your Mac, and your original recordings remain available for the final edit.

How to use CastCut.

  1. Add your recordings. Bring in the camera files and the audio from your podcast session.
  2. Review the automatic edit. Check the camera cuts, cleanup choices and suggested short clips.
  3. Export and finish. Continue in Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve. CastCut can also export finished MP4 videos.
