Tips for tutors

  • Bill per session or per package, and list the dates.
  • Address the invoice to the parent who pays, with the student's name in the description.
  • Note your policy on missed or rescheduled sessions.

What should a tutor invoice include?

Your name and contact details, the client, an invoice number, issue and due dates, one line per service with quantity and rate, the total in PHP, and how to pay you. Bill per session or per package, and list the dates.
