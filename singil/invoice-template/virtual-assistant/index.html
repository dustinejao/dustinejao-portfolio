Tips for virtual assistants

  • Bill by the hour or by a fixed monthly retainer, and say which on the invoice.
  • List the period covered, not just the hours: a client checks the dates first.
  • Most foreign clients pay through Wise or PayPal; put the exact account name and email.

Questions

What should a virtual assistant invoice include?

Your name and contact details, the client, an invoice number, issue and due dates, one line per service with quantity and rate, the total in USD, and how to pay you. Bill by the hour or by a fixed monthly retainer, and say which on the invoice.
