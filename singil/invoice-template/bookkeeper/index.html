Tips for bookkeepers

  • A fixed monthly fee per client keeps invoices predictable; list extra work as its own line.
  • Name the month the books cover, not the month you did the work.
  • State what is included in the monthly fee in your first invoice and keep it consistent.

Questions

What should a bookkeeper invoice include?

Your name and contact details, the client, an invoice number, issue and due dates, one line per service with quantity and rate, the total in PHP, and how to pay you. A fixed monthly fee per client keeps invoices predictable; list extra work as its own line.
