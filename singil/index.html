From payday to paid.
All in one place.

It knows the dates.

Biweekly (1st–15th, 16th–end of month), weekly or monthly. Singil suggests the next period and never makes you type a date. Arrows move it if you need to.

A PDF you'd send.

Clean type, your logo if you have one, your payment details at the bottom. A4, or US Letter for USD and CAD clients. The file is named for the client, not a string of numbers.

Old invoices stay put.

Each invoice keeps its own copy of who, what and how much. Change a client's rate next month and last month's invoice still says what you sent.

◫

A calendar for your billing.

See when clients are due, what you have sent, and what has been paid. Move through your year without losing the thread.

✓

Every invoice has a status.

Keep drafts, sent invoices and paid work in view. Check totals by currency in the year view.

Private by default.

No account or analytics. Your clients and invoices are stored on your device. A PDF leaves the app only when you choose to share it.

Questions

The short answers.

What is Singil?

Singil is an offline invoice maker for iPhone, made by Dustine Jao for freelancers who bill the same clients again and again. Save a client’s rate and schedule once, and Singil fills in the next invoice and creates a PDF to share.

Is Singil a subscription?

No. Singil is ₱99 once on the Philippine App Store, with no subscription and no in-app purchase. Prices in other countries follow the local App Store.

Does Singil work offline?

Yes. Making and saving invoices never needs a connection. You only need one to send the PDF by email or a messaging app.

Which currencies does Singil support?

PHP, USD, CAD, AUD, GBP, EUR, SGD and JPY. Invoices in USD or CAD use US Letter paper, and the others use A4.

Can I add tax, my logo and my GCash or bank details?

Yes. Turn on tax with your own rate and label, add your logo in Settings, and write your bank, GCash or Wise details under How to pay you. They print on every new invoice.

Which iPhones does Singil run on?

iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later.
