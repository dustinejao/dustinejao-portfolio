When a subscription app fits better

  • You need online payments on the invoice, team access, or accounting in one place: Zoho Invoice and QuickBooks do more.
  • You invoice from a computer or Android. Singil is iPhone-only.

When Singil fits better

  • You bill the same clients on a schedule, and want the next invoice ready in a tap.
  • You would rather pay ₱99 once than ₱449 or more every month.
  • You want client details and rates to stay on your phone, with no account.

Questions

Is there an invoice app without a subscription?

Yes. Singil for iPhone is ₱99 once on the Philippine App Store ($1.99 in the US). Of the nine invoice apps compared here, it is the only one sold as a one-time purchase; the rest are free to download with subscription plans.

Which invoice app does not collect data?

Singil's App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected". It has no account and keeps clients and invoices on the iPhone.
