Singil

Privacy

Singil collects nothing. There is no account, no server and no analytics, so there is nowhere for your invoices to go.

Updated 24 September 2026 · applies to Singil 1.0 on iPhone

In one line

Your clients and invoices stay on your phone.

There is no sign-in, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party analytics or advertising code in the app. I have no way of seeing your clients, your rates, your invoices or your payment details, because none of it is ever sent to me.

Singil does not open network connections to send your data anywhere. It works the same in airplane mode.

What Singil stores

On your device, and only there.

Your details. The name, email, address, logo and payment details you type into Settings, so they can be printed on your invoices.

Your clients. Each client's name, email, address, rate, currency, schedule and payment terms.

Your invoices. Every invoice you create, with its line items, dates, totals and status. Each invoice keeps its own copy of these details, so editing a client later never changes an invoice you already sent.

All of this is kept in a database in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. It is protected by your phone's own encryption while the phone is locked. Singil does not add a separate layer of encryption on top, and it does not claim to.

Photos

Only the pictures you pick.

If you add a logo or a client photo, Singil opens the system photo picker. You choose one image; Singil shrinks it and keeps a copy in its own storage, to print your logo on invoices or show the client's picture in your list. Client photos are never printed on invoices. Singil cannot see the rest of your library.

Both are optional. Singil never asks for your camera, contacts, location or microphone.

When you share an invoice

You choose where it goes.

Tapping Create & Share or Share PDF makes the PDF on your phone and hands it to the system share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you: Mail, Messages, a chat app, Files, a printer.

Once the PDF leaves through the share sheet, it is covered by the app or service you sent it with, not by this policy. The PDF contains exactly what you see on the invoice, including any payment details you added.

Backups

Your phone's backup, not mine.

Your iPhone's backup may include Singil's local data if you have backup enabled. Backup availability and restoration depend on your device settings and Apple's service. Singil does not run its own sync or backup server. Save important PDFs in a place you control.

What Apple sees

The purchase, not the invoices.

If Singil is purchased through the App Store, Apple handles the transaction. I may receive sales and diagnostic information through App Store Connect according to your device settings. I do not receive your invoice contents through Singil. Apple's handling of store and device data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Your rights

You already hold everything.

Because there is no server, there is nothing to request from me. Your data is on your phone:

  • To see it, open the app.
  • To take it elsewhere, share any invoice as a PDF.
  • To correct it, edit your details in Settings, or hold a client on the home screen and choose Edit Client.
  • To delete it, delete an invoice or a client in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

I cannot recover, delete or look at your data, because I never have it.

Children

Nothing collected from anyone.

Singil is a tool for making invoices. It collects no personal information from anyone of any age and has no ads, messaging or public content.

Changes

If this ever changes, it changes here first.

If a future version of Singil changes how data is handled, this page and the store privacy information will be updated before that version ships. The date at the top says when this page was last revised.
