How to invoice clients as a freelancer

By Dustine Jao, maker of Singil. Updated 27 November 2026.

A good invoice says who is billing whom, for what, how much, by when, and how to pay. Send it on the same day every billing period.

What goes on the invoice

  • Your name and contact details, and the client's name and billing contact.
  • An invoice number that only ever goes up: INV-001, INV-002. Never reuse one.
  • Issue date and due date. "Due in 15 days" is clearer as a date.
  • One line per service or period, with hours or quantity and your rate.
  • The total, in the currency you agreed, plus any tax with its label and rate.
  • How to pay you: bank details, GCash, Wise or PayPal. The fewer questions the client has, the sooner you are paid.

Getting paid on time

  1. Agree the schedule first. Weekly, every two weeks, monthly, or on delivery. Put it in writing before the work starts.
  2. Invoice on the same day every period. Clients pay on a rhythm; give them one.
  3. Make the due date obvious and keep it short: 7 to 30 days.
  4. Follow up the day after it is due, politely, with the invoice attached again.
  5. Track what is paid. Mark invoices paid as the money lands, so you always know who still owes you.

Questions

What should a freelance invoice include?

Your name and contact details, the client's name, a unique invoice number, the issue date and due date, a line for each service with quantity and rate, the total and currency, any tax, and how to pay you.

When should I send an invoice?

Agree it with the client up front: at the end of each billing period (weekly, every two weeks, monthly) for ongoing work, or on delivery for a one-off project. Sending on the same day every period is what gets you paid on a schedule.

How do I invoice a foreign client from the Philippines?

Bill in the currency you agreed, usually US dollars, and put payment details that work across borders, such as Wise or PayPal, on the invoice. Note the payment terms and due date in plain words.

Is an app invoice the same as a BIR invoice?

Not necessarily. A PDF invoice from an app or generator is a request for payment to your client. If you are registered with the BIR, ask your accountant which invoices must be BIR-registered.
