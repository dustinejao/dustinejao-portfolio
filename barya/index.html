Two taps
and it is logged.

Barya is loose change. Type the amount on the app's own keypad, tap what it was for, and the expense is on disk. No save button, no form, no keyboard sliding up, and nothing that ever leaves the phone.

The whole app is one screen.

What has gone today, how much of the budget that is, the number being typed, and the things it could have been. Everything else in Barya is somewhere you go on purpose.

The dots are the budget: filled is spent, empty is what is left. Top row today, bottom row this month, readable without a single numeral.

Entry

A keypad, not a form.

The keys are always on screen, so there is no box to tap first and nothing reflows mid-entry. It does the sum too: split a bill as 1200 ÷ 4 and settle the answer.

Receipts

Point it at the total.

The camera reads the amount using the recogniser already on your phone. It knows to ignore the cash you handed over and the change you got back, which are usually the biggest numbers on the slip.

Statements

A month in one go.

Import a bank or card statement as a PDF, typed or scanned. Every transaction it finds is listed for you to check, and only the ones you keep ticked are written.

Privacy

There is no server.

No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no network calls anywhere in the code. The ledger is encrypted on the phone with a key held in the Keychain or the Android Keystore.

Under it

Built to be boring in the right places.

Offline first

Every read and write is synchronous and local, so an expense is saved before your finger leaves the glass. It works in airplane mode because there is nothing for it to reach.

Your categories

Start with five, rename the list to whatever you actually spend on, and put your favourites first. Make a new one from the entry screen and it logs to it in the same tap.

Budgets that remember

Change this month's budget and last month keeps its own. Budgets are a history, not a number, so the ledger never rewrites whether a past month was over or under.

Yours to take

Export to CSV or JSON whenever you like. A backup rides along with your iCloud or Google account, so a new phone picks up where the old one left off.

Readable

Four text sizes, three faces for the numbers, true black on OLED, and bold that follows your phone's own accessibility setting instead of adding another switch.

Barya is still being built. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Android today; the store listings are not up yet. If you want it on your phone before then, say so and I will send it over.
