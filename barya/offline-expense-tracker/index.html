An expense tracker that works offline

By Dustine Jao, maker of Barya. Updated 27 November 2026.

Barya is an expense tracker for iPhone that never touches the internet. Logging, receipt scanning, bank statement import, budgets and charts all run on the phone, so it works in airplane mode, with no load, and with the SIM out.

What works offline

  • Logging: a keypad with arithmetic and one tap to file.
  • Receipt scanning with the text recognition built into iOS. The photo is deleted after it is read.
  • Statement import from a PDF you saved, typed or scanned, with every row shown before anything is written.
  • Daily and monthly budgets, a ledger by day, and charts over 14 days, 90 days or 12 months.
  • Backups as JSON and exports as CSV, saved where you choose.

Why it is built this way

An expense log is a list of where you were and what you bought. Barya keeps it encrypted on the phone and nowhere else: no account to breach, no server to subpoena, no analytics SDK. It is also why it is fast; nothing waits on a connection.

Questions

Is there an expense tracker that works without internet?

Yes. Barya for iPhone makes no network requests at all: logging, receipt scanning, statement import, budgets and charts all run on the phone. It works in airplane mode and with the SIM out.

How do I back up an offline expense tracker?

Barya exports a JSON backup that restores onto a new phone, and a CSV for any spreadsheet. Save the file wherever you keep backups.

Does Barya need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no email and no bank connection. Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode can lock the app.
