    Making it to week 52

    • Pick a day. Payday or Sunday. Save on that day every week, before spending.
    • Keep it out of reach. A separate savings account or digital wallet you do not spend from.
    • Start in reverse if the end of the year is tight. Big weeks in January, small ones in December.
    • Missed a week? Tick any unticked week when you can. The order matters less than finishing.
    • Watch where the rest goes. The challenge works when the daily spending fits around it; a daily budget is what makes room.

    Questions

    What is the 52-week ipon challenge?

    A savings habit: in week 1 you set aside a small amount, and each week you add the same amount again. With ₱20, week 1 is ₱20, week 2 is ₱40, and week 52 is ₱1,040. After a year you have ₱27,560.

    How much will I save with the ₱50 ipon challenge?

    ₱68,900 in 52 weeks: ₱50 in week 1, rising by ₱50 each week to ₱2,600 in week 52. The ₱100 version saves ₱137,800.

    What is the formula for the 52-week challenge?

    Base amount × 1,378, because 1 + 2 + … + 52 = 1,378. ₱20 × 1,378 = ₱27,560.

    Is it easier to do the challenge in reverse?

    Many people find it so: start with the biggest week in January, when 13th month pay is still around, and finish with the smallest in December, when gifts and Noche Buena eat the budget. The total is the same.

    What is the 365-day ipon challenge?

    Save ₱1 on day 1, ₱2 on day 2, up to ₱365 on the last day, for ₱66,795 in a year. You can tick the days in any order.
