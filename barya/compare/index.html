What sets Barya apart

  • Two taps. It opens on a keypad. Type the amount, tap the category, done. It does arithmetic in the field, so 1200 / 4 splits a bill.
  • Receipts and statements, read on the phone. Point it at a receipt total, or open a bank or card statement PDF, typed or scanned. Nothing is uploaded.
  • No network at all. No account, no bank connection, no analytics. It works with the SIM out.
  • Paid once. No subscription and no tier above it.

When another app fits better

  • You want to track accounts, net worth or investments, not just spending: Tarsi covers more ground.
  • You want shared budgets with a partner or sync across devices: look at apps with an account and cloud sync, and accept what they collect.
  • You are on Android. Barya is iPhone-only.

Questions

What is the best expense tracker app in the Philippines without a subscription?

If you want to pay once: Barya is ₱49 and Cashtra ₱149 on the Philippine App Store, and neither collects any data. Tarsi and Money Lover also sell lifetime upgrades, at ₱1,999 and ₱1,290. Barya logs an expense in two taps and reads receipts and PDF bank statements on the phone.

Which expense tracker apps do not collect data?

On the Philippine App Store, Barya, Cashtra and Tarsi declare "Data Not Collected" on their privacy labels, checked on 27 November 2026.

Is there an expense tracker that does not connect to my bank?

Yes. Barya never connects to a bank. To bring in a month of spending, it reads a PDF statement you saved, on the phone, and shows every row before importing it.
